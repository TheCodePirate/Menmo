How conversations stay compliant

Do we qualify for the Clean Energy District grant if our solar array reaches 1.2MW and includes community job training?

You meet the capacity threshold (minimum 1MW) and the workforce development priority, but you still need the city council’s net-zero endorsement letter before applying.Clean Energy District Grant Brief §2.1Workforce Addendum §4.3

What documents should I collect for the endorsement?

Assemble the sustainability roadmap, emissions baseline, and last quarter’s impact report. The endorsement template on file lists them as mandatory attachments.City Net-Zero Resolution Checklist p.3Impact Report Q4 FY23
